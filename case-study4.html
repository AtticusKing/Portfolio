Slapstik Brand Refresh

A full transformation of identity, tone, and presentation across print and digital assets.

The Challenge

Slapstik’s identity was misaligned and scattered. The logo didn't scale, pitch decks lacked hierarchy, and the tone felt more accidental than deliberate. We needed to build a unified brand system that still felt bold and fun.

1000 × 650
Old Brand Assets

Inspiration & Tone

We drew from editorial design, absurdist humor, sticker culture, and loud, confident type systems.

Logo Evolution

The new mark preserves Slapstik’s wit and energy, but gives it a sharper, more recognizable shape and simplified palette.

Pitch Deck Transformation

We rebuilt the deck from scratch—introducing visual anchors, spacing systems, and CTA slides that reflected the tone of the brand.

Typographic structure and color blocking improved information clarity and attention retention across long decks.
